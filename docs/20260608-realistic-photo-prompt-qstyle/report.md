# 写实自然照片风格提示词辅助报告

> 本报告将 AI 图像生成中的"写实自然照片风格"进行系统化整理，提取可直接复用的提示词模板与风格组合建议。

## 核心目标

生成"看起来像真实生活中随手拍下来的照片"：
- 人物自然、不僵硬、不像摆拍
- 具有强烈写实感和现场感
- 重点是真实可信，而非完美构图

## 核心英文术语

| 中文表达 | 英文术语 | 适用场景 |
|---|---|---|
| 随手拍风格 | snapshot aesthetic | 最贴近"像随手拍"的感觉 |
| 自然抓拍 | candid photography | 强调人物自然、不看镜头、不摆拍 |
| 纪实写实风 | documentary-style realism | 强调真实现场感、生活痕迹 |
| 生活方式摄影 | lifestyle photography | 强调日常场景中的自然人物状态 |
| 手机原相机感 | natural smartphone photo | 强调像手机直接拍出来 |
| 照片级写实 | photorealistic | 强调生成效果像真实照片 |

**最佳组合**：`photorealistic candid snapshot, documentary-style, natural smartphone photo`

## 画面特征

1. 人物不正面对镜头硬笑，表情像自然发生
2. 构图可以略微不完美——轻微偏移、裁切、歪斜
3. 光线接近日常自然光，而非影棚光
4. 场景保留生活细节：街道、咖啡杯、衣服褶皱、杂物背景
5. 可以有轻微运动模糊、噪点、压缩感或手机摄影质感
6. 人物处于动作过程中：走路、回头、低头、整理头发、看窗外、喝咖啡
7. 整体像真实照片，而不是AI精修海报
8. 纪实写实感 + 生活方式摄影 + 手机原相机感三者融合

## 推荐提示词结构

> 主体 + 场景 + 动作状态 + 拍摄风格 + 光线 + 相机质感 + 排除项

## 可复用模板（4个）

### 模板一：自然人像随手拍
`photorealistic candid snapshot of a person in an everyday scene, unposed, natural expression, looking away from the camera, casual body language, documentary-style realism, natural smartphone photo, available light, realistic skin texture, imperfect composition, slight background clutter, real-life moment, not a studio portrait, not overly polished`

### 模板二：街头快照感
`realistic street candid photo, snapshot aesthetic, a person walking on the street, captured mid-motion, slightly off-center framing, natural daylight, casual expression, documentary photography style, smartphone camera look, subtle motion blur, realistic environment, unposed, authentic everyday moment`

### 模板三：生活方式纪实感
`documentary-style lifestyle photo, photorealistic, casual everyday moment, person interacting naturally with the environment, soft natural light, unposed expression, realistic details, slight imperfections, candid composition, natural smartphone photography, not commercial, not fashion editorial, not studio shot`

### 模板四：非摆拍特写
`photorealistic close-up candid portrait, natural expression, looking slightly away from camera, relaxed face, subtle emotion, real skin texture, natural light, shallow depth of field, documentary realism, unposed, intimate real-life moment, not glamour retouching, not artificial studio lighting`

## 负面提示词（排除项）

`overly polished` / `studio lighting` / `glamour shot` / `fashion editorial` / `plastic skin` / `airbrushed skin` / `fake smile` / `stiff pose` / `over-posed` / `cinematic poster` / `hyper-stylized` / `doll-like face`

## 最终推荐完整提示词

`photorealistic candid snapshot, documentary-style realism, natural smartphone photo, unposed subject, natural expression, casual everyday moment, imperfect composition, available natural light, realistic skin texture, slight motion blur, subtle background clutter, authentic real-life atmosphere, not studio lighting, not overly polished, not fashion editorial, not artificial pose`

## 风格组合建议

| 组合 | 关键词 |
|---|---|
| 最推荐 | photorealistic + candid snapshot + documentary-style realism + natural smartphone photo |
| 偏人像自然 | candid portrait + unposed + natural expression + real skin texture |
| 偏街头纪实 | street candid + documentary photography + snapshot aesthetic |
| 偏生活日常 | lifestyle photography + casual everyday moment + natural light |
| 偏手机真实感 | natural smartphone photo + imperfect composition + slight motion blur |

## 一句话总结

> 照片级写实的自然抓拍快照风格，融合了 snapshot aesthetic + candid photography + documentary-style realism + natural smartphone photo 四大特点。
