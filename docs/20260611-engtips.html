§03
CEFR 等级

欧洲语言共同参考框架

参考 CEFR 3.3 Global Scale，为学习者提供科学的水平参照

A1 初学者

Can understand and use familiar everyday expressions and very basic phrases.

A2 基础

Can communicate in simple and routine tasks and very short social exchanges.

B1 中级

Can deal with most situations likely to arise while travelling. Can produce simple connected text.

B2 中高级

Can interact with a degree of fluency and spontaneity with native speakers.

C1 高级

Can express ideas fluently and spontaneously without much obvious searching for expressions.

C2 精通

Can understand with ease virtually everything heard or read. Near-native fluency.
