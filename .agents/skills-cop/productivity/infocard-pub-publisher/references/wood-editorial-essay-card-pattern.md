# Wood editorial essay card pattern

## Trigger
Use the `wood` theme when the source is a long-form essay, commentary, or editorial-style post about agentic engineering, loops, harnesses, responsibility, or nuanced workflow tradeoffs.

## Why wood fits
- Warm paper / editorial feel matches reflective essays better than punchy poster themes.
- Serif hero titles give the card a magazine-like voice.
- Blue links and brown callout blocks work well for quoted arguments and citations.
- Black flow bars and compact sectioning keep dense reasoning readable.

## Session-tested signals
Applied successfully to:
- Armin Ronacher, "The Coming Loop"

## Practical checklist
- Lead with the thesis, not the site name.
- Use a pull quote for the strongest line.
- Separate harness / agent / human responsibilities clearly.
- Keep long argumentative sections in 3–6 short blocks.
- Preserve the paper tone on mobile; do not over-darken the page.

## When not to use
- Pure tool catalogs
- Monitoring dashboards
- Highly promotional landing pages
- Cards whose main value is a UI demo rather than a written argument
