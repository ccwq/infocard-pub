# Informal Framework Source Boundaries

Use this note when a card explains a named technique plus a later internet-originated classification, quantification, or maturity model.

## Required split

Separate claims into two layers:

1. **Underlying concept**: what the named technique actually means and which parts have independent documentation or established usage.
2. **Later model**: who proposed the L1-L5 (or similar) interpretation, when it appeared, what it measures, and whether it is formal, standardized, academic, or only a community proposal.

Never let a namesake in the technique title silently inherit authorship of a later scale.

## Evidence labels

Use explicit labels such as:

- `已核实：` supported by the primary source or independent authoritative documentation.
- `来源作者提出：` attributed to the person/post that introduced the later model.
- `未发现统一标准：` when searches do not identify a standards body, official rubric, or peer-reviewed consensus.

Do not claim “official”, “scientific scale”, “standard level”, or “Feynman himself proposed” without a primary source.

## Card structure

Prefer this order:

1. Boundary sentence near the hero.
2. Model/level explanation.
3. Misconception correction.
4. Source and evidence-boundary section naming the original post/article and retrieval date.

## Review checklist

- Is the named technique distinguished from the later classification?
- Is authorship of the later model attributed?
- Is the model's status (formal / informal / unstandardized) visible?
- Are examples presented as examples, not universal definitions?
- Are unresolved claims left marked rather than completed by inference?
